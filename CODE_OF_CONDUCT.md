# Code of Conduct

PRC (Packing Race Coordinator) exists to help volunteers run Race to Feed, a charity event that packs food for people who need it. The people who work on it, report problems with it and use it on event day are giving their time. This is how we treat each other.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are volunteers, and many are not programmers. Explain things in plain words. Nobody should feel foolish for asking.
- **Assume good intent.** A blunt bug report or a clumsy change is usually someone trying to help in a hurry.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** Say what you think is wrong with an idea and why. Don't make it about who suggested it.
- **Respect the event.** Decisions about how a race is run belong to the organisers. Suggest, explain, and accept the answer.
- **Protect people's information.** Team names, referee names, email addresses and anything else about real participants or volunteers stays out of issues, screenshots and examples. Use made-up names.
- **Give credit.** Thank people for reports, fixes and reviews.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about anyone's age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or level of experience.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexual language and images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Accusing a volunteer, referee or team of cheating or bias in public. The fairness report and warnings in the app are prompts for the organisers to look at; they are not proof of anything. Raise a concern privately with the organisers.
- Deliberately disrupting an event, including testing against the live site while an event is running.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages) and anywhere someone is speaking or acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour here makes you uncomfortable, or you see something that breaks this code, tell the maintainer privately: [development@weissaudio.app](mailto:development@weissaudio.app).

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. You will get a reply, and you will not be penalised for reporting in good faith. If your concern is about the maintainer, you can report it to GitHub directly using the "Report content" option on the comment or profile.

Security problems are a separate matter: follow `SECURITY.md`.

## What happens next

The maintainer will look at what happened and may, depending on how serious it is:

1. Talk to the person privately and ask them to stop.
2. Edit or remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions.
3. Block the person from the repository, for a time or permanently.
